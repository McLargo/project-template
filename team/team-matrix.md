# Team matrix

This document is a template to record the skills of the team members, trying to
identify the gaps and the areas of improvement. It can be used in terms of
skills or technologies, but also in terms of domains (different services or
areas of the business).

## Skill matrix

| Name | Role | Skill 1 | Skill 2 | ... |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| John Doe | Software Engineer | :star: :star: | :star: | ... |

## Domain matrix

| Name | Role | Domain 1 | Domain 2 | ... |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| John Doe | Software Engineer | :star: :star: :star: | :star: | ... |

Stars can go from 1 to 5, being 1 the lowest level of knowledge and 5 the
highest.
