# Glossary

Have a common glossary of terms is important to ensure product and developers
talks in the same language. This usually is a living document and unique to each
project.

See some examples of terms that can be included in the glossary

- **ADR** - Architecture Decision Record. Document where the architectural
  decisions are recorded, including the context, the decision and the
  consequences.
- **DoD** - Definition of Done. Document where the criteria that must be met
  for a task to be considered done are recorded.
- **DoR** - Definition of Ready. Document where the criteria that must be met
  for a task to be considered ready to be worked on are recorded.

> List of terms should not be just technical terms, but also business terms.
